=====================================================
BLAST FURNACE GUILD - SHIFT HANDOVER GUIDE
Document ID: DOC-SOP-HANDOVER-01 (Rev 3.0)
=====================================================

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
   - Establishes standardized procedures for transferring operational responsibility
     between incoming and outgoing plant crews.
   - Applies to all shift supervisors, furnace operators, and maintenance staff.

2. SHIFT ROTATION SCHEDULE
   - Morning Shift:  06:00 - 14:00 (Supervisor: Geoff Eligah)
   - Afternoon Shift: 14:00 - 22:00 (Supervisor: Benjamin Natas)
   - Night Shift:    22:00 - 06:00 (Supervisor: John Deeler)

3. MANDATORY HANDOVER PROCEDURES
   - Physical Walkthrough: Outgoing supervisor must conduct a joint walk with the incoming
     supervisor 15 minutes prior to shift end.
   - Furnace Status Verification: Document operational state for Furnaces 1, 2, 3, and 4
     (Running / Idle / Under Maintenance / Shutdown).
   - Work Order Signoff: Record completed batch quotas and outstanding work orders.
   - Safety & Incident Log: Record zero-accident confirmation or attach incident reports.
   - Digital Log Entry: Submit the handover note via Blast Furnace Guild Intranet -> Shift Handover.
