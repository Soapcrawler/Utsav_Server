=====================================================
SASTRA MANUFACTURING - EMERGENCY RESPONSE GUIDE
Document ID: DOC-EMG-RESP-03 (Rev 5.0)
=====================================================

1. EVACUATION PROTOCOL
   - When the continuous plant horn sounds, immediately shut off machinery if safe to do so.
   - Walk (do not run) toward the nearest lighted EXIT door.
   - Assembly Point 1: Main Employee Parking Lot (Grid Alpha-3).
   - Assembly Point 2: South Gate Receiving Area (Alternate).
   - Supervisors must conduct immediate roll-call using shift attendance logs.

2. FIRE SUPPRESSION PROCEDURES
   - Electrical Fires (Class C): Use CO2 or Clean Agent extinguishers.
   - Molten Metal Fires (Class D): USE DRY POWDER EXTANGUISHERS ONLY.
   - CRITICAL WARNING: NEVER spray water or foam onto molten slag or liquid metal.
     Doing so causes an explosive steam expansion hazard!

3. CHEMICAL EXPOSURE & INJURY PROTOCOLS
   - Immediate eye/skin flush: 15 minutes minimum continuous flush at safety stations.
   - Plant Paramedic On-Duty Extension: 2222.
   - Regional Hospital Emergency Direct Line: 911 / (555) 019-4820.
   - Building 2 Decontamination room key is kept in the Shift Supervisor lockbox.
